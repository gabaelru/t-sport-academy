# ⚽ Registration is now open for the new Toquero Sport Academy season!

At **Toquero Sport Academy**, we believe every player has great potential waiting to be discovered.

Our mission is to guide children and young players through their football development using a modern, structured methodology adapted to every stage of learning. We help them improve their technical, tactical, physical and mental skills.

Beyond sporting performance, we promote essential values such as **commitment**, **respect**, **teamwork**, **discipline** and **self-improvement**, creating an environment where every player can grow both on and off the pitch.

## What do we offer?

- ⚽ Individual and group football coaching and technical training.
- 🏃 Development of football-specific physical abilities.
- 🧠 Decision-making and game intelligence training.
- 🎯 A methodology adapted to each player's age and skill level.
- 👨‍🏫 Qualified coaches committed to the complete development of every player.
- 📈 Personalised monitoring of each player's progress.

## 📍 Training locations

Training sessions will take place **Monday to Thursday** at two leading sports facilities in **Vitoria-Gasteiz**:

- 🏟️ Fundación Estadio.
- 🏫 Olabide Ikastola.

Groups, schedules and training venues will be organised according to the players' age and category.

## 📅 Season duration

The season will run **from 5 October to 3 June**, following the academy's official calendar.

Through the **360Player** app, players and families will be able to access:

- 📅 Training sessions.
- 🗓️ Schedules and calendars.
- 📢 Squad selections.
- 🎉 Events.
- 💬 Academy communications.
- 📲 All information related to academy activities.

## 🚀 A new season begins!

Registration is now open with exciting new features and the same commitment to providing high-quality training, helping every player reach their full potential.

> Developing great football players is our goal, but developing great people is our greatest achievement.
